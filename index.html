MEET THE PRODUCT

What Nordic is.
What you buy.

Nordic Gaming Browser is a Chromium-based browser for Windows, designed for web games with optimized performance, customization and additional tools for HaxBall.

Browser access

We offer paid access to Nordic Gaming Browser and its included features: browser and Windows settings, themes, resolution, account levels and HaxBall tools such as FPS, keyboard and chat controls and replay analysis.

You can review each group of features in the product description.

A personal-use license

You buy temporary access to the browser, linked to your Discord account. The monthly plan allows one device and one simultaneous session.

You need 64-bit Windows, a Discord account and an Internet connection to sign in and validate access. Check your device's compatibility before paying.

BUY WITH CONFIDENCE

Refunds
and cancellations.

Last updated: September 18, 2026.

Refund within 10 days

You can request a full refund within 10 calendar days of payment, even if you have already activated access. This is our commercial policy and does not limit your legal rights.

Delivery, access or billing problems

If you were charged twice, we did not deliver access or the product has a problem we cannot resolve, we will review your case to fix it or refund the corresponding amount. You can also submit these requests after the 10-day period.

How to request a refund

Use the contact form or write to noordicdev@gmail.com. Include the purchase date, transaction ID if available, your Discord username and a brief explanation. Do not send passwords or full card details.

Response and refund

We will respond within 2 business days. Once approved, we will initiate the refund within 5 business days, preferably through the original payment method. When the credit appears depends on the bank or provider; we will keep you informed of its status and any necessary alternative.

What happens to the license

A full refund cancels the access associated with that purchase. If you only want to stop using Nordic at the end of the period, you do not need to cancel future charges: the plan does not renew automatically.
